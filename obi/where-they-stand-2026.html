THE ELECTION BRIEF · NOVEMBER 2, 2026

Where They Stand

What the 33 physicians on the November 3 congressional ballot have said or done on the AOA's 2026 federal priorities, in their own framing and with a source for each item. Omega Beta Iota does not endorse candidates.

U.S. Senate

Mike Katz, MD (R)

Delaware · Challenger
  • Coverage and costs Calls for funding rural health initiatives that put specialists and primary care physicians within reach of Delaware residents. drmikekatz.com
  • Telehealth Supports expanding telehealth and access to care for veterans living in rural communities. drmikekatz.com
  • Coverage and costs Said at a candidate forum that the Affordable Care Act has good elements but overall has not improved quality of or access to care. whyy.org

Roger Marshall, MD (R)

Kansas · Incumbent
  • Prior authorization Led Senate reintroduction of the Improving Seniors' Timely Access to Care Act to streamline Medicare Advantage prior authorization through electronic processes. marshall.senate.gov
  • Physician payment Introduced S. 1640, the Medicare Patient Access and Practice Stabilization Act of 2025, adding an 8.51 percent Medicare physician payment increase for 2025. congress.gov
  • Telehealth Joined in introducing the CONNECT for Health Act, which would expand Medicare telehealth coverage and remove location limits on telehealth services. marshall.senate.gov

Abdul El-Sayed, MD (D)

Michigan · Open seat
  • Coverage and costs Supports Medicare for All, saying it would separate coverage from employment, remove high out of pocket costs, and cost less than the current system. michiganpublic.org
  • Public health Criticized HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s management of HHS and said opposing him alone is not enough to rebuild public health. abdulforsenate.com

Annie Andrews, MD (D)

South Carolina · Challenger
  • Coverage and costs Calls for increasing incentives to recruit and retain health care workers in rural areas. drannieandrews.com
  • Coverage and costs Pledges to protect and expand Medicaid coverage. drannieandrews.com
  • Coverage and costs Calls for increased funding for federally qualified health centers and rural hospitals. drannieandrews.com

U.S. House: Seeking a First Term

Zuhdi Jasser, MD (R)

AZ-04 · Challenger

Shimon Blau, MD (I)

CO-01 · Open seat
  • Prior authorization Criticizes insurer denials of physician-ordered care and peer-to-peer appeals, saying they raise costs, and calls for holding insurance companies accountable. denverite.com

Samir Witta, MD (I)

CO-06 · Challenger
  • Coverage and costs Calls for capping out-of-pocket deductibles at $2,000. ballotpedia.org

Amy Chai, MD (R)

CT-01 · Nominee

Michael Goldstein, MD (R)

CT-04 · Challenger

John Cowan, MD (R)

GA-11 · Open seat
  • Coverage and costs Campaigned on fixing problems with the Affordable Care Act. wabe.org

KY-06 · Open seat

  • Public health Opposes vaccine mandates and supports public health funding, with more of it directed to states. insurancenewsnet.com

Anil Kumar, MD (I)

MI-11 · Open seat

Mike Eisenhauer, MD (I)

MT-02 · Challenger
  • Coverage and costs Pledges to protect rural and critical-access health care, saying recent federal Medicaid changes threaten Montana's rural health facilities. projects.montanafreepress.org

  • Coverage and costs Says he does not think a single-payer system will work. projects.montanafreepress.org

Steven Feldman, MD (L)

NJ-12 · Open seat

Daniel Cottam, MD (L)

UT-02 · Challenger

How this guide was compiled. For sitting members of Congress, we reviewed bills sponsored or cosponsored in the 119th Congress and official statements. For other candidates, we reviewed campaign websites, candidate surveys and reporting by news outlets. Each candidate received the same review regardless of party. Up to three items are shown per candidate, chosen for relevance to physicians and students, so a candidate's full record may be broader. A candidate with few or no items may not have published positions on these topics. Party labels are listed as they appear on the ballot (D Democrat, R Republican, I Independent, L Libertarian). Information is current as of November 2, 2026. Questions or corrections: omegabetaiota.org.
